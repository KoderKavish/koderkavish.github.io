Mapping

How an account gets mapped before anyone writes copy

Most of the lift above came from this layer. When replies were weak, the fix was almost always in targeting and data, not the email. For Union, each account goes through the same six steps and only gets a message once every step has a sourced answer.

1 Signal→ 2 Property + portfolio→ 3 Leasing stack read from page code→ 4 Buyer by role→ 5 Proof that fits→ 6 Sequence to demo

Pilot math

What a 4-week pilot should produce

Benchmarked on Aios rates, with a floor at half those rates since Union is a new ICP for me. Move the sliders to match the volume you'd want. This is a model, not a promise; week 2 data replaces it.

Floor = 1.05% touch-to-meeting and 22% meeting-to-SQL. Benchmark = 2.1% and 33%. Light bar is the floor, dark bar is the benchmark, on a square-root scale so the small stages stay visible.

Pilot shape

Four weeks, reported weekly

  1. Week 1Signal feed for Ohio, Florida and NorCal. Map 360 accounts. Domains warmed, CRM fields agreed with your AEs.
  2. Week 2Launch 3 plays. First read on reply rate by play and by buyer role.
  3. Week 3Cut the weakest play, double the strongest. OPTECH 48h follow-up runs.
  4. Week 4Demos booked, held and sourced pipeline by play. Recommendation on the role.

Weekly report: touches, replies, positive replies, demos booked, demos held, by play and territory.
