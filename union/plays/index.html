2. A 30-day pilot: three plays for Union

Built around the AE territories you're hiring for (Ohio, Florida and Northern California) and the operators on the Union page. Each play has a map, a message, and one number it's judged on.

Play 1: Lease-up front doors

Map
Every community that opened or started pre-leasing in the last 90 days in the three territories, from trade press and operator sites. For each one, read the page code for its leasing stack (EliseAI, Knock, Funnel, Entrata and others), its office hours and how tours get booked. Then name the person who owns lease-ups at the operator.
Message
Their own page, mapped: every way a renter reaches the community and which tool catches it. Already tested on Lewis's Independence in West Sacramento.
Judged on
Demos booked with operators in lease-up.

Play 2: Takeovers and management mergers

Map
Acquisitions where a third-party manager is named, plus management-company mergers, when renter data moves between tools anyway. Matched to the state rules that change what each lease must offer (for example, California's rent-reporting offer).
Message
What the new property owes its residents, drafted for them, and how Union carries it on one record. Already tested on Bidwell Park in Fremont.
Judged on
Demos booked with presidents and VPs of property management.

Play 3: OPTECH before and after

Map
The operators in the three territories likely to be at OPTECH (Nov 16-18), each with its page audit already done.
Message
Before: a meeting ask that leads with their audit. After: follow-up within 48 hours for everyone your team met, carrying what was discussed.
Judged on
Meetings booked at the event, and post-event demos.
