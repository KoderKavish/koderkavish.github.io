Doubling meetings with a one-person outbound engine

Aios Medical / Fella Health (YC W20/S21) · Founding GTM BDR · Feb 2025 – Feb 2026

Problem
When I joined as one of the earliest GTM hires, reply rates were low and a team of 5+ BDRs was booking 6–7 meetings a week. There was no repeatable outbound infrastructure underneath them.
Approach
Treat outbound as an infrastructure problem: fix targeting and data upstream before touching copy, then run structured experiments on messaging, segmentation and targeting across email and LinkedIn.
What I built
Signal-based outbound with enrichment, persona segmentation and automated sequencing. Multi-domain email infrastructure, targeting on funding, hiring and tech-stack signals, and the SOPs and playbooks later hires scaled from.
Result
  • 46 meetings booked a month: about 12 a week, up from the 6–7 a week the 5+ BDR team was booking
  • $500K+ qualified pipeline in 6 months
  • Up to 15 SQLs a month from 500+ touches a week
  • Reply rate 5% → 12%, with LinkedIn replying even higher than email
  • A new vertical opened within the first 6 weeks

A Clay operating system for executive search

Madison Wells, boutique retained executive search firm · NeuroScale Ventures · Fractional GTM Engineer

Problem
Client acquisition and candidate sourcing were manual: researchers worked LinkedIn and spreadsheets one role at a time, and candidates were judged on a binary Great Fit / Bad Fit call.
Approach
Two parallel Clay tracks, one for business development and one for candidate sourcing, both driven by signals instead of static lists.
What I built
  • Five hiring signals: multiple similar open roles, hiring-velocity spikes, executive departures, funding events, and roles stuck open 60+ days
  • A pre-posting layer that catches demand before the job goes live: leadership changes, M&A / PE activity, headcount velocity
  • A 4-tier account framework (current client, past client, strategic target, prospect) with tier-based outreach routing and signal-matched copy
  • A weighted 100-point candidate score with plain-English explanations, replacing the binary fit call
Result
Sourcing, business development and internal execution now run on one scalable Clay framework instead of manual processes, and the firm is keeping me on going forward.

“Kavish was able to customize a Clay operating system for us that really helps streamline sourcing, business development and operational workflows for Madison Wells, rather than just relying on manual processes like we were in the past. We now have a more scalable framework that supports how we execute retained search.”

Alex Bell, Owner, Madison Wells · watch the video

Targeting real agent deployment, not ‘uses AI’

AI security / agentic access monitoring startup, US · NeuroScale Ventures · Jul 2026 – present

Problem
Book qualified meetings with security and AI platform leaders across fintech, insurance and NBFI companies. Almost every company claims to use AI, so targeting on that alone produces noise.
Approach
ICP and signal scoring built around evidence of real agent deployment. Paid per qualified meeting, with the bar set past title and attendance.
What I built
  • Signals: hiring for agent infrastructure roles, GenAI-risk language in job descriptions, security JDs mentioning AI agents or MCP, and detectable LangGraph, CrewAI or Bedrock usage
  • Account and contact lists sourced and scored live in Deepline
  • Competitive research on three competitors, feeding the positioning
Result
An enterprise outbound motion from zero, priced on qualified meetings only.
