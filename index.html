About

About

The fix was almost always upstream.

I started in GTM running x97, an outbound and content agency for SaaS and VC-backed startups, where I learned the full sales cycle with my own clients and my own pipeline.

In February 2025 I joined Aios (YC W20/S21) as Founding GTM BDR, built the outbound engine from scratch, and then led the company's international expansion. That seat changed how I think: when replies were weak, the fix was almost always in targeting and data, not in the email.

Today I work as a fractional GTM engineer with B2B companies across AI, SaaS and digital health, building enrichment waterfalls, scoring, deliverability and CRM attribution that each client owns after handoff. I also coach for Clay's Cohort Compass program and run GTM Engineer Decodes, a LinkedIn group of 5,000+ members.

Delivery stack
  1. Signal capturehiring language, leadership changes, technographics, growth
  2. Account scoringone buying-intent score, so reps work accounts with a reason to buy
  3. Decision-maker identificationClay + LLM reads LinkedIn, sites and org structure
  4. Enrichment11-provider waterfall, deduplicated, cheapest source first
  5. Sending infrastructuredomains, warm-up, inbox rotation, deliverability monitoring
  6. AttributionCRM reporting the client owns after handoff

Six layers. Fix the first two and the copy gets easy.
