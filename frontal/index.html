03 If I join

Three things I'd build at Frontal.

1. A public-records signal library, built one vertical at a time

A catalog of registries with per-account, dated records (state liquor and trade licenses, CMS ownership changes, permits) wired into Clay as sources. Each vertical client's Foundation then starts from a live trigger, like Santé's 175 pending stores, not a static list.

2. Job-board signals for every client's TAM, written into the CRM

A daily read of Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever boards for each client's scored accounts: seller cohorts, stack-naming roles and hiring surges, pushed to HubSpot or Salesforce with decay and synced to ad audiences, so outbound and ABM act on the same signal.

3. Frontal's own pipeline: a mini Foundation for every new raise

A weekly feed of B2B rounds from Series A to C, matched to the founder or head of growth, each sent a slice of its own TAM scored by a live signal, exactly like this page. It turns Frontal's offer into the first email.
